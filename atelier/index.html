The Heritage

In Chitral, shu is made the long way: the sheep are washed in the streams, shorn, the wool carded and spun, warped outdoors, and woven on a wooden loom over twenty-odd days. The cloth is foot-fulled in the hot springs at Garam Chashma, then dried in the sun. Northora’s pieces begin from that documented craft — nothing here is invented, and every piece is a concept study until its making is verified.

Atelier prototype — not the live site

What this proves

The design language of a great luxury house — cream ground, serif headlines, uppercase sans, square buttons, quiet grids, generous whitespace — rebuilt with Northora’s own content.

What was adapted — and what was not copied

The design system is the reference house’s; everything in it is Northora’s. Their name, emblem, orange, images, product names, copy, prices, cart and checkout were not taken. Northora stays request-first: no prices, no bag, no checkout — every piece is a labelled concept study and links to the real enquiry form. The reference uses video heroes; Northora has no licensed Chitral footage, so the hero is a real photograph with a slow zoom.
