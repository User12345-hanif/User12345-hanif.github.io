Lookbook prototype — not the live site

What this proves

The video’s design system, rebuilt with NORTHORA’s own content: dark showcases with a silver giant word and a tall product overlapping it, huge bold-sans headings, white cards with tilted side cards in the collection carousel, black pill buttons, and a white page.

What was adapted — and what was not copied

The design is the video’s; everything in it is NORTHORA’s. No Brandify branding, product names, images, prices or copy were reused, and their cart and checkout were deliberately left out — NORTHORA stays request-first, and every piece links to its concept study and to the real enquiry form. The one thing the video has that this page cannot fake is full-body model photography: NORTHORA has no model shots, so the concept studies stand in, labelled as concept studies. Model photography is a separate decision — a labelled concept shoot, or real photography — not something to invent silently.
