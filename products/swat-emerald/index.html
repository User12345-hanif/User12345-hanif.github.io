Swat Emerald

Rough emerald from the Mingora mines.

Emeralds have been mined at Mingora in Swat since 1958 — deep green stones of high clarity, documented as exported rough to Dubai and Thailand for cutting, and often sold onward as Indian or Jaipur emerald. What leaves Swat is rough stone; the cutting, and much of the value, happens abroad. This is a concept study in the rough stone — not a stocked item, and no origin is certified.
